## Usage Tips

1. **Match Job Descriptions**: Use exact terminology from job postings
2. **Context Matters**: Embed keywords in achievement statements
3. **Acronyms + Full Terms**: Include both "CI/CD" and "Continuous Integration/Continuous Delivery"
4. **Quantify Impact**: Combine keywords with measurable results
5. **Stay Current**: Update keywords based on emerging technologies
